Combat in a top-down game lives or dies on feedback. This week I worked on three small things.

## Three changes

A brief hit pause when a strike lands, a short screen shake scaled to the damage, and a slightly
tighter dodge window so a good dodge feels earned.

Each change is tiny on its own. Together they make hits feel heavy.

> If the player cannot read the attack, the fight is not fair.

Next: enemy telegraphs, so players can read an attack before it lands.
