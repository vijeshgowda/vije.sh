Every autumn the motorcycle world quietly moves a year forward. The model-year pages
flip over, the press kits land, and suddenly a bike you could buy in the spring is
"last year's". This log is my attempt to make sense of **what riders will actually be
choosing between in 2027**, across every category I could reasonably cover: from
superbikes and adventure bikes to cruisers, motocross racers, electric bikes and
scooters.

That is twelve categories, three motorcycles in each: thirty-six bikes in total. They are
not rankings. Each category gets three different answers to the same question, because
the "best" bike depends on the job you give it.

**Research log, 16 October 2026:** this is an editorial guide built from the
manufacturers' own websites, not a hands-on group test, and I have not ridden every bike
here. EICMA is still a few weeks away, so not every manufacturer has published its 2027
range yet. I have labelled each bike honestly:

- **New for 2027** or **2027 listing:** the manufacturer's site already sells or presents
  it as a 2027 model.
- **Current model, carrying into 2027:** the site still shows a 2026 (or earlier) model
  year. These are bikes you will realistically find in showrooms through 2027, but the
  specification may change when the new model year is published.

Figures are manufacturer claims, quoted in the units of the page I read (US pages in
pounds and inches, UK pages in kilograms and millimetres). Model years, equipment and
availability vary by country. The photographs are official manufacturer images; where a
picture comes from an earlier model year's material, the caption says so. Full sources
and photo credits are at the end.

## What is actually new for 2027

Strip away the colour changes and three themes stand out from the pages I read.

1. **Competition and off-road bikes moved first.** Motocross and enduro machines
   traditionally launch their next model year early, and 2027 is no different: KTM,
   Husqvarna and Yamaha all have 2027 race bikes online, and Suzuki's reborn DR-Z4S
   and Husqvarna's FE 501S already wear 2027 badges.
2. **Electronics keep filtering down.** Launch control and traction control are now
   ordinary on 450 motocross bikes. A 400 cc dual-sport (the DR-Z4S) gets three power
   modes, four traction-control modes and switchable ABS. KTM's big adventure bike
   adds a portrait touchscreen.
3. **The headline road launches are big-ticket bikes.** Ducati's new Panigale V4 and
   KTM's reworked 1390 Super Adventure S EVO are the most significant 2027 road-bike
   pages so far. For most categories, though, the 2027 decision will be made between
   current models, which is why I've included them.

## Superbikes and supersports

Full-fairing sport bikes ask the most of their riders and the most of the budget for
tyres, insurance and track days. My three cover the spectrum: a flagship V4, a
circuit-bred inline four, and a middleweight that does not demand a race licence to
enjoy.

### Ducati Panigale V4

![Red Ducati Panigale V4 in a studio side profile on a black background](./ducati-panigale-v4-1.webp "Ducati Panigale V4, studio side view from Ducati's 2027 model page. Photo: Ducati.")

![Red Ducati Panigale V4 three-quarter studio view showing the winged nose and fairing](./ducati-panigale-v4-2.webp "Panigale V4 three-quarter view. Photo: Ducati.")

![Red Ducati Panigale V4 front three-quarter view showing the headlights and side fairing](./ducati-panigale-v4-3.webp "Panigale V4 front three-quarter view. Photo: Ducati.")

**2027 status:** new for 2027. Ducati's US site presents it as the "New Panigale V4 2027".

The Panigale is the reference point for the category. Ducati lists a **1,103 cc V4 with
209 hp at 12,750 rpm** and 89 lb-ft (121.3 Nm) at 11,250 rpm, a six-speed gearbox with
Ducati Quick Shift up/down 2.0, Öhlins suspension with Ducati Electronic Suspension 3.0,
and a track-day kit of Ducati Power Launch, lap timer and pit limiter. Seat height is
850 mm.

**Who it's for:** an experienced rider who wants a road-legal superbike and plans to
make track days the centre of ownership. On the road, 209 hp is mostly a number you pay
to insure.

### Yamaha YZF-R1

![Blue Yamaha YZF-R1 on a race circuit with its winglets and Brembo brakes visible](./yamaha-yzf-r1-1.webp "Yamaha YZF-R1 on track, from Yamaha's 2026 model page. Photo: Yamaha Motor.")

**2027 status:** current model, carrying into 2027. Yamaha's US site lists the 2026 YZF-R1.

The R1 is still built around Yamaha's 998 cc crossplane CP4 inline four, with a
crankshaft concept that Yamaha traces to its YZR-M1 MotoGP bike. The current version
adds carbon-fibre winglets to the Deltabox chassis, alongside YCC-T ride-by-wire, an
IMU-based rider-aid package, Brembo front brakes and high-specification KYB forks.

**Who it's for:** someone who wants a litre-class, circuit-minded sport bike with full
road equipment, and enjoys the character of a crossplane four as much as the lap time.

### Triumph Daytona 660

![Two Triumph Daytona 660 motorcycles in a dramatic studio scene](./triumph-daytona-660-1.webp "Triumph Daytona 660, launch imagery from Triumph's 2024 model year. Photo: Triumph Motorcycles.")

![Triumph Daytona 660 cornering on a mountain road, side view](./triumph-daytona-660-3.webp "Daytona 660 cornering; launch imagery from the 2024 model year. Photo: Triumph Motorcycles.")

**2027 status:** current model, carrying into 2027. Triumph's site still shows the 2025 model.

The Daytona 660 is the sensible end of this category, which is exactly why it is here.
Triumph builds it around its triple, tuned for low-down torque as well as a strong top
end, with Sport, Road and Rain riding modes that change throttle response, ABS and
traction control. Triumph's own pitch is focused handling combined with all-day comfort.

**Who it's for:** a newer or returning sport-bike rider who wants a full fairing and a
proper sporting feel without a track-only riding position or superbike running costs.

## Naked bikes and streetfighters

Take the fairing off a sport bike and you get the most popular kind of performance
motorcycle on the road. These three range from a superbike with handlebars to a
lively, everyday triple.

**Who it's for:** an assertive road rider who wants superbike power and brakes, and
accepts the wind blast that comes with them. If the Panigale's model-year change
filters into the Streetfighter, expect an update. Check before buying.

The Street Triple is the naked bike that can moonlight as a track-day tool. Triumph's

pays off if you use it.

## Touring and sport-touring

Touring bikes are judged on the motorway, in the rain and on day five, not on the first
twenty minutes. The three here are a full luxury tourer, a sporty triple with luggage,
and a faired Ducati that leans more towards sport.

### Honda Gold Wing

![Honda Gold Wing ridden at sunset with its fairing and panniers visible](./honda-gl1800-gold-wing-1.webp "Honda GL1800 Gold Wing on the road. Photo: Honda Motor Europe.")

**2027 status:** current model, carrying into 2027. Honda UK lists it in the 2026 range.

Nothing else here travels quite like a Gold Wing. Honda's 1,833 cc flat six makes
93 kW and **170 Nm at 4,500 rpm**, through a standard seven-speed Dual Clutch
Transmission. Four riding modes (Tour, Sport, Econ and Rain), double-wishbone front
suspension and a 7-inch TFT with wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto complete a
specification that reads more like a car's.

**Who it's for:** two-up riders covering serious distances who value effortless
progress and comfort over low weight.

Yamaha calls it "sport touring simplified", and that is fair. The Tracer 9 uses the
same 890 cc CP3 triple as the MT-09, with YCC-T ride-by-wire, an aluminium frame, KYB

multi-day trips, without paying for a heavyweight tourer.

The SuperSport is the sport end of sport-touring: a full fairing and Ducati handling,
with a more forgiving riding position than a Panigale. Ducati lists a 937 cc engine
with **110 hp at 9,000 rpm** and 69 lb-ft (93 Nm) at 6,500 rpm, ride-by-wire, Ducati
Quick Shift up/down and an 810 mm seat.

**Who it's for:** a rider who wants a faired Ducati for long days and is willing to add
luggage, rather than starting with a dedicated tourer.

The biggest-selling premium category, and the one with the most electronics per
kilogram. All three of these are tall, powerful and expensive to specify fully. Sit on
them before you fall for a brochure.

This is the most technology-heavy bike in the log. KTM's 2027 page describes a

For many riders this is the sweet spot: lighter, cheaper and less intimidating than the
big bikes, yet still capable of crossing a continent. These are three different
philosophies.

## Motocross

Competition bikes are where model years really mean something: the 2027 race bikes are
already on sale. All three are 450s, the premier class, and none is road-legal.

## Which category is yours?

Start with the riding you do most weeks, not the trip you might take once.

```mermaid
flowchart TD
  accTitle: Find your 2027 motorcycle category
  accDescr: Mostly road riding leads to naked, sport, touring, cruiser or retro bikes. Dirt riding leads to adventure, dual-sport or motocross. City riding leads to scooters, small bikes or electric. Every route ends with a fit, licence and budget check.
  A["What will you mostly ride?"] --> B["Paved roads"]
  A --> C["Gravel and trails"]
  A --> D["Town and commuting"]
  B --> B1["Fast and sporty: superbike or naked"]
  B --> B2["Long distance: touring or big adventure"]
  B --> B3["Relaxed: cruiser or retro"]
  C --> C1["Travel with luggage: middleweight adventure"]
  C --> C2["Trails first: enduro or dual-sport"]
  C --> C3["Racing: motocross"]
  D --> D1["Practical: scooter"]
  D --> D2["Learning: small-capacity bike"]
  D --> D3["Short, regular trips: electric"]
  B1 --> E["Check fit, licence, insurance and total cost"]
  B2 --> E
  B3 --> E
  C1 --> E
  C2 --> E
  C3 --> E
  D1 --> E
  D2 --> E
  D3 --> E
```

In words: road riders choose between sport, touring and relaxed bikes. Riders heading
off-road choose by how much luggage and road time they need. Town riders pick a
scooter, a small bike or an electric. Then let fit, licence category and total cost
decide.

## Frequently asked questions

### Should I wait for the 2027 model?

Only if a confirmed change matters to you. Most bikes in this log carry over largely
unchanged, so a well-priced outgoing model can be the better buy. For the bikes that
really are new (the Panigale V4, the 1390 Super Adventure, the DR-Z4S and the 2027
motocross bikes), compare the new specification against discounts on the outgoing
model.

### Why are so many bikes "carrying into 2027"?

Because it is October. Many manufacturers publish their next model year after the
autumn shows, and some only change colours. I would rather label a bike honestly than
guess what its 2027 page will say.

### Why are there no prices?

Prices vary by country, taxes, trim and dealer offers, and they change during the year.
Get local quotes for identically equipped bikes, including on-the-road costs.

### Which of these is the best first motorcycle?

Look first at the small-capacity section, the CRF300L and the Himalayan, but training,
licensing and fit come first. Nothing in this log replaces an instructor's advice or a
test ride.
